## (a) Top 10 issues, ranked by impact

### 1. Case hero title in difference blend is illegible over photography and UI captures
- Shots: `v2/case-sri-krishna-sagar-0.png`, `v2/case-armoriq-platform-0.png`, `v2/case-shopstr-0.png`, `v2/case-sri-krishna-sagar-intro-mid.png`
- Where: `.case__head` / `.case__title` (sticky, `mix-blend-mode: difference`, `color: var(--paper)`), the first frame of every case page.
- What is wrong: difference inverts per pixel, so over mid-luminance imagery (laptop keyboards, wood desks, grey UI chrome) the glyph fill lands near 50% grey, with a contrast ratio near 1:1 against its own surroundings. Inside a single word the fill shifts colour letter by letter (SRI pale blue, KRISHNA olive brown, SAGAR rainbow), and image detail (keycaps, chart bars, legend text "Medium / Low") shows through the counters. The page's largest type has the worst legibility on the site. It is also the first frame after the curtain, which is the most important frame in the case flow.
- Fix: take the title out of difference while it sits over the hero. Set `.case__head { mix-blend-mode: normal; color: var(--on-cobalt); }` while the hero is in view. Add a bottom scrim to `.case__hero-scrim`: `background: linear-gradient(to top, rgba(15,15,14,0.62) 0%, rgba(15,15,14,0.28) 38%, transparent 62%)`. That gives paper text at least 7:1 over the bottom 40% of any cover. If difference has to stay for the compact state (issue 2), switch on a class when the hero leaves the viewport rather than blending from frame one.
- Owner: C (`styles/case.css`, `views/case.ts`).

### 2. The compact sticky case title collides with body copy and turns yellow over cobalt
- Shots: `v2/case-armoriq-platform-2.png` (over the "What I did" text), `v2/case-armoriq-platform-3.png` (olive yellow over the cobalt gallery), `v2/case-armoriq-platform-4.png` (over the Result row), `v2/case-shopstr-2.png`, `v2/case-sri-krishna-sagar-2.png`, `v2/case-sri-krishna-sagar-3.png`
- Where: `.case__title` after the shrink, fixed at y ~36, centred, for the whole length of the page. The header is hidden by then, so this title is the only chrome.
- What is wrong: it is a free-floating 24px uppercase title with no surface behind it. Body text, captions and Result rows scroll straight through it, which gives text-on-text overprint (for example "ARMORIQ PLATFORM" over "system, so a decision gets checked"). Over the cobalt gallery band, difference renders paper minus cobalt as roughly `#c7be0a`. That is an off-palette yellow, and it breaks the one-accent rule.
- Fix (pick one, and the first is better):
  1. When the hero bottom crosses the top edge, mask the compact title out (`yPercent: -130`, 0.5s expoInOut). Bring it back on the same scroll-up that reveals the header, and set it in the header row in place of the nav.
  2. Keep it pinned, but give it a surface: a 72px paper band, `background: var(--paper); border-bottom: 1px solid var(--line)`, with `mix-blend-mode: normal` and ink text. On `[data-cursor-tone="cobalt"]` bands, swap to `background: var(--cobalt); color: var(--on-cobalt)`.
- Owner: C (`styles/case.css`, `views/case.ts`). If the title moves into the header row, A (`styles/chrome.css`).

### 3. Case hero chrome, subtitle and counter overprint the screenshot's own UI text
- Shots: `v2/case-shopstr-0.png` (header nav sits on Shopstr's "Search by name, price, or seller"), `v2/case-armoriq-platform-0.png` (counter "01 / 16" runs into "2/5 ready", subtitle sits on "AI systems / 2 of 10", breadcrumb "Acme Health / Overview" directly under the site nav), `v2/case-armoriq-platform-landed.png` (subtitle on "Awaiting review / 3 / 12"), `v2/case-sri-krishna-sagar-0.png` (clock over the laptop bezel)
- Where: `.case__hero` full bleed under the difference-blended `.chrome__bar`; `.case__caption` subtitle and counter at the hero's bottom edge.
- What is wrong: covers that are product UI captures carry their own nav bars, breadcrumbs and numerals. With no scrim and no inset, site chrome and image chrome read as one layer, and the eye cannot tell which nav is the site's. The subtitle is white 24px serif italic over a mostly white dashboard, roughly 1.3:1.
- Fix: the issue 1 bottom scrim also covers subtitle and counter. Add a top scrim of `linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(15,15,14,0.45), transparent 140px)`, and set `data-cursor-tone`/chrome tone to paper (non-difference) while the case hero is under the bar. For UI-capture covers (armoriq-platform, armortools, ivish), inset the media 88px from the top so the capture's own top bar never sits under the site header.
- Owner: C (`styles/case.css`) for the scrims and inset; A (`styles/chrome.css`, `core/chrome.ts`) for the chrome tone switch.

### 4. Off-palette yellow cursor: phase goes idle before the curtain has left, and hover-cobalt text is not tagged
- Shots: `v2/tx/plain-07.jpg` (yellow 16px square over the exiting curtain), `v2/contact-2-hover.png` (yellow dot over the cobalt email)
- Where: `core/transition.ts` sets the phase to `idle` while the sheet is still at about -78% (`-734px` at 900 tall). `styles/cursor.css` only paints the cursor paper during `covering|revealing` or `.is-on-cobalt`. On /contact/ the email turns cobalt on hover, but its surface is not `[data-cursor-tone="cobalt"]`.
- What is wrong: difference over `--cobalt` yields yellow. The cursor CSS already names this exact problem and guards against it, so these are the two holes in that guard.
- Fix: in `transition.ts`, keep `data-phase="revealing"` until the sheet tween's `onComplete` and release hover blocking separately; `is-blocking` can stay at `sheetAt + len * 0.5`. In `cursor.ts`, resolve `is-on-cobalt` from the hovered element's computed `color` or `background-color` matching `--cobalt`, not only from the attribute. Or add `data-cursor-tone="cobalt"` to `.ct__email` while hovered.
- Owner: A (`core/transition.ts`, `core/cursor.ts`). B can tag `.ct__email` in `templates/contact.ts` as a stopgap.

### 5. Cover crops: `object-fit: cover` into near-square frames chops baked-in copy and UI
- Shots: `v2/home-200.png` (VerbX: "To make cross-language communication s" cut at the frame edge; the cursor label collides with it), `v2/home-100.png` (ArmorIQ: "e Health", "Board summa", "77 ope" sliced), `v2/work-01-hover.png`, `v2/case-shopstr-2.png` (buyer journey board cut at "4. DECIDE Online Pa"), `v2/case-shopstr-3.png` (design system board cut mid-swatch row)
- Where: `.works__media` (home stack, frame 798x770, aspect 1.04) and `.wk__cover` (`aspect-ratio: 4/5`). Covers are 1.41 to 1.60 landscape, so the crop removes 26% to 38% of width.
- What is wrong: these are authored artefacts with text inside them. A crop that cuts a sentence mid-word looks like a mistake, not an editorial crop. Where the image has a safe zone the cover crop is fine (Shopstr laptop, Social posters on black).
- Fix: drive the fit from `media.json`. When `|frameAspect - mediaAspect| / mediaAspect > 0.2`, render `object-fit: contain` on a `var(--paper-2)` well with `padding: 6%` (the Social content tool frame on ink already works this way). Alternatively, add a `focus` field per shot and map it to `object-position`. For VerbX use `object-position: 22% 50%` or a different shot. On the case gallery, give `case__mod` images their natural aspect (`aspect-ratio: w / h` from media.json) instead of row-matched heights.
- Owner: B (`styles/home.css`, `templates/home.ts`), E (`styles/work.css`, `templates/work.ts`), C (`styles/case.css` gallery).

### 6. Work index: the cover preview is stuck on at rest and floats off the list after filtering
- Shots: `v2/work-00.png` (ArmorIQ cover visible with nothing hovered, covering the row rules of rows 01 to 08), `v2/work-07-filtered.png` (Systems filter leaves 3 rows; the 450x560 cover hangs 345px below the last row over empty paper)
- Where: `templates/work.ts` marks cover 0 `is-active` on render; `.wk__cover` is absolutely positioned at `top: 0` of the lane with no clamp to the list height.
- What is wrong: bybang's preview is a hover affordance, so showing it at rest reads as a stuck state. After a filter, the frame no longer belongs to any row: it is an orphaned image, and the composition looks broken.
- Fix: render with no `is-active` cover. Reveal on `pointerenter` of the list and wipe out (`clip-path: inset(0 0 100% 0)`, 0.5s expoInOut) on `pointerleave`. Clamp the cover's y to `min(rowTop, listBottom - coverHeight)` and recompute on filter. If the list is shorter than the cover, scale the cover to the list height (`height: min(560px, listHeight)`).
- Owner: E (`templates/work.ts`, `views/work.ts`, `styles/work.css`).

### 7. Cursor rect takeover ignores the target's corner radius
- Shots: `v2/work-07-filtered.png` (square ink box around the pill "Systems 03", which inverts to a light pill inside a dark rectangle), `v2/cursor-switch.png` (square box around the pill NOW switch)
- Where: `.cursor__shape` takeover state, `border-radius: var(--r-xs)` fixed in `styles/cursor.css`.
- What is wrong: a 3px-corner rectangle around a 999px-radius pill gives a double contour with mismatched corners. The takeover is meant to become the element, and here it visibly does not.
- Fix: in `cursor.ts`, on takeover read `getComputedStyle(target).borderRadius` and set it as `--cursor-r` on the shape. Transition `border-radius` on the same `--cursor-morph` duration and `--ease-back` curve that is already declared.
- Owner: A (`core/cursor.ts`, `styles/cursor.css`).

### 8. Case body sub-columns leave the 12-column grid

The Problem two-up directly above starts at 262 / 844 (on grid). The Decision three-up uses the same off-grid split. The Result list ends at 1178 (column 10) while every other block ends at 1410.

- Fix: for three-up blocks use the subgrid and explicit spans `3 / span 3`, `6 / span 3`, `9 / span 3`, which start at 262.5 / 611.25 / 960 and leave column 12 as air. Do the same for `.case__decision` cols. Set `.case__result` to `grid-column: 3 / -1` so its rules end at 1410 like the Problem rules.

### 9. The thin case page (sri-krishna-sagar) reads as filler

### 10. Mono labels fail contrast site-wide
- Shots: any page. Clearest on `v2/home-00-hero.png` (ROLE / BASED / STATUS, SCROLL), `v2/about-4.png` (EXPERIENCE, EDUCATION), `v2/case-shopstr-3.png` (gallery captions), `v2/home-100.png` (inactive row kinds)
- Where: `--mute: rgba(15,15,14,0.48)` on `--paper` gives about `#868580`, 3.3:1, on 11px uppercase mono. `--faint` (0.28) gives about 1.9:1 and is used for dimmed row text.
- What is wrong: 11px text needs 4.5:1 for WCAG AA. Labels carry the IA here (section names, meta keys, counts, captions), so they cannot be decorative.
- Fix: `--mute: rgba(15,15,14,0.62)` (about 5.2:1). Keep `--faint` for rules and dimmed display type only. Inactive rows' mono kinds and years should use `--mute`, not `--faint`, so dimmed rows stay readable at 11px.
- Owner: Orchestrator (`styles/tokens.css`). E and B consume it.
